TIGERS

The tiger (Panthera tigris) is the largest living cat species and a member of the genus Panthera. It is native to Asia and is known for its orange coat with black stripes, powerful build, and excellent hunting skills.

Tigers inhabit a range of ecosystems including tropical forests, grasslands, and mangrove swamps. They are primarily carnivorous and mostly hunt large mammals such as deer and wild boar. Tigers are solitary animals and are known for their strength, speed, and stealth.
