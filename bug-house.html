BUG HOUSE

Step into the amazing world of insects. The Bug House is home to a variety of fascinating insects from around the world. You can see beetles, butterflies, stick insects, spiders and many more.

Our interactive displays allow you to learn about insect life cycles, habitats, and the important role insects play in our environment.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND IT?

The Bug House is located near the Children's Area, next to the Exhibition Hall.
