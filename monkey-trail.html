MONKEY TRAIL

Walk through the Monkey Trail, a scenic forest trail that winds through natural woodland. The trail gives visitors a chance to see our playful monkeys and learn about their behaviour and natural habitat.

Look out for the different monkey species as they climb, swing and play in the trees. Information signs along the trail help you learn about the forest, the monkeys and the important role they play in our environment.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND IT?

The Monkey Trail is located to the north of the main entrance, through the forest area.
